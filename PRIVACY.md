# Privacy Policy — Beatmeat

_Last updated: 24 July 2026_

Beatmeat ("the app") is a musical instrument app. **It does not collect, store,
or share any personal data.**

- **No accounts, no sign-in.** The app never asks for your name, email, or any
  personal information.
- **No analytics, no advertising, no tracking** of any kind. No third-party
  trackers or SDKs that profile you.
- **Content downloads.** To play, the app downloads its scenes (audio and pad
  layouts) over the internet from a public repository. These are ordinary
  requests for app content — they are not used to identify you — and the content
  is cached on your device for offline playback.
- **Nothing leaves your device.** The app does not upload any information about
  you or your activity.

Because the app collects no personal data, there is nothing for you to access,
correct, or delete.

**Children.** The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the latest version will always be available
at this page.

**Contact.** Questions? Open an issue at
<https://github.com/jhnmeelr/bm-export>.
